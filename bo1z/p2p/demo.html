bo1z p2p — serverless transport demo

Two peers, no lobby server, no matchmaking, no relay, no backend. Each side produces one blob; you carry it across by hand. That exchange is the rendezvous and the authentication: the DTLS fingerprint rides inside the blob, so whoever hands you the blob is provably whoever you end up connected to.

1 · Session

STUN never carries game data — it only tells a peer its own public address. It is needed when the two players are on different home networks. On one machine or one LAN, leave it at none and nothing outside this page is contacted at all.

3 · Host-authoritative movement

Move with WASD or the arrows. Your own square is client-side predicted, so it responds instantly; the other player is drawn from interpolated snapshots, deliberately 100 ms in the past so a 30 Hz stream with jitter still looks smooth. Only the host simulates — the guest sends inputs and renders what comes back.

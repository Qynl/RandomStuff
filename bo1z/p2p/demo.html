bo1z p2p — four players, no server

One player hosts; up to three friends join. Every connection is made from text you copy between chat windows: there is no lobby server, relay or account. Each invite carries its own WebRTC offer and the host's DTLS fingerprint, so whoever gives you an invite is the one you connect to.
Movement uses WASD or the arrow keys. This is a movement sandbox, not Black Ops Zombies.
